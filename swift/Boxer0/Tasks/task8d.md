## Input and arbitration

Added a76x46point Guard touch surface alongside Jab/Cross in the bottom gameplay row. It reuses HoldTouchSurface/HoldTouchView, which capture touches until release or cancellation even when a finger slides outside the control. Multiple fingers on one surface keep it held until the last releases; controls own independent touch sets. Guard is not a toggle. Its physical guardHeld state is separate from actual GuardPlayback state/effectiveness.

Guard has its own input-generation token. Movement/punch input resets therefore do not accidentally discard a still-held Guard finger during punch completion or nonfatal reaction. Scene inactivity/teardown, Reset, defeat and Blue Rise explicitly clear the held flag, guard/recoil models and generation; an old finger cannot re-arm protection afterward. Pause freezes guard state but still allows a deliberate release. Entering guard while paused presents frame1 and waits for scene-time advancement.

Guard pressed during an active blue punch is remembered but cannot interrupt the punch. After completion, a held Guard starts frame1; release before completion prevents entry. During raising/raised guard, Jab/Cross eligibility is false. A nonfatal HIT cancels protection and recoil while preserving the separate physical hold; guard entry can restart after hit reaction finishes. Newly restarted entry does not consume that reaction-completion tick. Defeat cancels the hold entirely, and knockdown/Rise cannot be interrupted by it.

## Movement and visuals

Movement-button input is retained while guarding, so a held Advance/Retreat resumes on guard release without a fresh press (unless an existing separate punch, HIT, pause or lifecycle cancellation cleared movement input).

Blue guard/recoil textures have priority over ordinary idle/footwork textures, below legitimate hit-reaction and knockdown/Rise. Blue BLOCK uses the existing120ms Jab/160ms Cross physical recoil playback with its blue palette variants. Feet/root/scale/facing remain unchanged. Releasing Guard cancels recoil and effective protection immediately; no completion callback can restore it later.
